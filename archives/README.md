## Archive rules

1. Preserve code, frozen controls, measurements, missing-data accounting, limitations, and conclusions.
2. Distinguish Complete from Closed: a decision to stop does not fulfill a measurement requirement.
3. State the conditions for reopening closed or paused work.
4. Keep historical material here and keep active surfaces focused on current work.
5. Preserve negative and inconclusive evidence with the same care as positive results.
