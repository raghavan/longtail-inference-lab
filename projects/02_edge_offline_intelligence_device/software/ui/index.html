    Interface prototype. The traces are generated, not measured — there is no microphone, no speech recognition, and no model behind this page. It exists to test how the device reads at a glance: whether you can tell listening from thinking from speaking without a screen full of text. Stage names match the real ledger in experiments/01_spoken_latency_and_residency.md, and the status lamp follows the colour vocabulary in the design direction. Cutting the microphone overrides everything, which is the one behaviour the finished device must guarantee in hardware.
